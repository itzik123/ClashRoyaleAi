ClashRoyaleEnv

A fast, deterministic Clash Royale battle engine for reinforcement learning.
A full match in ~10 ms on one CPU core
132 cards and 41 Evolutions, in C++17
Recurrent PPO, self-play, and lookahead search
C++17 · pybind11 · Python · MIT license
The opponent scores each play by simulating 10 s ahead
