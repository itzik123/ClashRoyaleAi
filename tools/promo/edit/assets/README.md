# Assets for edit_short.py

Nothing here but this note is committed: your recording, music and font stay
on your machine.

| file | what | needed? |
|---|---|---|
| `voiceover.mp3` | your recorded voiceover (.wav, .m4a also work) | for the final cut; without it the edit is a silent preview |
| `music.mp3` | a music bed you have the rights to (e.g. the YouTube Audio Library) | optional |
| `Montserrat-Black.ttf` | the caption font ([Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Montserrat), free) | optional; Arial Black stands in |
| `sfx/<name>.wav` | your own sound effect for `<name>`: replaces a built-in one (`whoosh`, `pop`, `impact`, `ding`) or adds a new name you use in `short.json` | optional |
